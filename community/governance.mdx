Latchway uses maintainer stewardship with transparent technical records.
Repository permissions and public organization membership appropriate to each
role are the authoritative membership record.

## Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Contributor | Propose focused changes, tests, documentation, and review feedback |
| Maintainer | Merge changes and uphold compatibility, security, quality, and documentation gates |
| Security maintainer | Receive private reports and coordinate assessment, remediation, and disclosure |
| Release maintainer | Verify cross-repository artifacts, provenance, signatures, publication, and completion evidence |

No role grants permission to bypass required reviews, protected environments,
security checks, or evidence gates.

## Decisions

Routine implementation decisions use pull-request review. Durable or
security-sensitive decisions use an [ADR](/community/adrs). Maintainers seek
consensus; when consensus cannot be reached, a repository owner records the
decision and rationale in the relevant ADR.

Conflicts of interest must be disclosed. A materially affected maintainer
should recuse from final approval. Security reports stay within the private
advisory process until coordinated disclosure.

## Releases

A release maintainer may publish immutable product tags, GHCR images, and SDK
packages only after the exact candidate passes its protected prepublication
promotion gates. The completion record remains `release_ready: false` until
clean public consumers and exact registry bytes pass post-publication
conformance; only then may the finalizer authorize release-qualified claims. A
source version, local test, tag draft, package archive, or provider smoke test
is not sufficient by itself.

Version 1 requires compatible core, JavaScript, iOS, Android, and React Native
coordinates plus the generated public-documentation mirror. Current status is
published on [Release status](/release-status).

## Governance changes

Governance changes require a documented public proposal. Once two maintainers
exist, they require at least two maintainer approvals and a public review
period of at least seven days. Until then, the repository owner may approve a
change but must record the rationale.

## Participation

Follow the project [contribution guide](/community/contributing) and the
[Code of Conduct](https://github.com/Latchway/latchway/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
Use public issues only for non-sensitive bugs and focused proposals. Send any
suspected vulnerability through [Security reporting](/community/security-reporting).
